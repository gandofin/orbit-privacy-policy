# ORBIT Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 1 November 2026**

**Gandofin** · gandofin@gmail.com

## 1. About ORBIT and the developer

ORBIT is a casual arcade game developed and published under the name Gandofin. This Privacy Policy explains what information ORBIT stores on your device, what information it sends to online services when you use connected features, why that information is used, and how you can contact the developer.

Privacy contact: gandofin@gmail.com.

## 2. Information stored on your device

ORBIT stores game information locally on your device. Depending on how you play, this can include game progress, stars, achievements, missions, unlocked or selected items, settings such as language and sound, local score history, and the best Classic score and run duration saved for later leaderboard synchronisation.

ORBIT also stores a local diagnostic crash report when an unexpected error occurs. The report is kept on the device. It is not automatically sent to the developer. You may choose to copy it and send it yourself.

If you uninstall ORBIT or clear its app data, locally stored information may be deleted, subject to Android backup and restore settings described below.

## 3. Information sent to ORBIT's online services

When you use the Global Leaderboard or Feedback features, ORBIT communicates with Supabase, which provides the game's anonymous authentication and database services. Network communication with the Supabase project uses HTTPS.

• Anonymous player account: ORBIT creates an anonymous Supabase account. Its account identifier and authentication/session tokens are used to recognise the same player between sessions. The account identifier and session credentials are stored locally by the app.
• Global Leaderboard: ORBIT sends the anonymous player identifier, the username you choose, and your best Classic-mode score and run duration so the leaderboard can display and rank records.
• Feedback: if you submit feedback, ORBIT sends the message you wrote, the feedback category, your anonymous player identifier, the game version, device manufacturer/model, and Android version. Do not include sensitive personal information in feedback.

The app does not ask you to provide an email address, real name, phone number, contacts, precise location, photos, or payment-card details. Information you voluntarily type into a feedback message may, of course, contain personal information, so please avoid including it.

## 4. Android backup and device transfer

The current Android backup rules include the app's anonymous authentication/session preferences and the locally cached best Classic score and run duration. Android cloud backup or device-transfer features may therefore restore the online player identity and that cached leaderboard record when Android and your Google account settings permit it. The app's other game progress and settings are not included in the backup rules currently shipped with this version.

Android backup and restore are provided by Google and are subject to your device and Google account settings.

## 5. Advertising and third-party services

The app includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK. In the current release configuration, production ads are disabled; test banner ads may appear in debug/test builds. If advertising is enabled in a later release, Google may process device and advertising-related information, such as an advertising identifier, IP address, app interactions, and diagnostic information, as described in Google's own policies and disclosures.

Supabase processes the information described in section 3 to provide anonymous authentication, database, and leaderboard/feedback services. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy practices. ORBIT does not sell your personal information.

## 6. Purchases and gift codes

Google Play Billing is not connected in the current release build, so ORBIT does not currently process real-money in-app purchases or receive payment details. Star-based items and game progress are handled locally. Online gift-code redemption is not active in this version; entered voucher codes are not accepted until an online validation service is implemented.

If real-money purchases or active advertising are introduced in a future version, this Policy and the app's Google Play disclosures will be updated before those features are enabled.

## 7. Crash reports and sharing

Crash reports are stored locally and are shown to you in the app when available. If you choose the Copy action, the report is copied to your device clipboard. ORBIT does not automatically upload that report. If you choose to share a score or the app using Android's share function, the text you choose to share is passed to the app you select.

## 8. How information is used

Information is used only to operate the game's connected features: maintaining an anonymous player session, saving and displaying leaderboard records, receiving feedback, diagnosing problems when you voluntarily send a crash report, and maintaining the app's local gameplay features. ORBIT does not sell personal information or use the leaderboard username as a real-world identity verification method.

## 9. Retention, access, and deletion requests

Local game data remains on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app, except for information that Android backup may retain or restore as described above.

Online account, profile, leaderboard, and feedback records are retained in the Supabase project until they are deleted or are no longer needed for the purposes described in this Policy, subject to applicable legal or security requirements. To request deletion of your anonymous ORBIT account and associated online data, email gandofin@gmail.com. Include your ORBIT username and explain that you are requesting deletion. Do not send passwords, authentication tokens, or other sensitive information. The developer may ask for reasonable information to identify the relevant anonymous account before processing the request.

Important: the current app version does not yet provide a self-service account-deletion control inside the app. This is a separate implementation item that must be completed before claiming full compliance with Google Play's account-deletion requirements. This email contact is the current manual request channel; it is not an in-app deletion feature.

## 10. Children

ORBIT is a general-audience arcade game and is not specifically directed to children under 13. The developer does not knowingly request personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through ORBIT's online features, contact the developer at gandofin@gmail.com.

## 11. Security

ORBIT uses HTTPS for communication with the Supabase service. The app uses an anonymous public client key; access to server data is intended to be controlled by server-side policies and functions. No method of internet transmission or electronic storage is completely secure, and the developer cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 12. Changes to this Policy

This Policy may be updated when the app's features, service providers, or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date. If a future app release adds advertising, purchases, analytics, or other data collection, the Policy and relevant Google Play disclosures will be updated accordingly.
